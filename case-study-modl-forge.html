Modl Forge — turning a technical workflow into a guided journey.

A web platform that helps users generate, upload, clean, analyze and prepare datasets for machine-learning model training.

01 — Problem

ML workflows are powerful, but easy to get lost in.

Machine-learning workflows can be confusing for users who need to manage datasets, cleaning, quality analysis and model training in multiple steps — often without knowing which step they're on or why it matters.

02 — Design Goal

A guided workflow for beginners that stays efficient for experts

Create a guided workflow that makes the process understandable for beginners while remaining efficient for technical users who just want to move fast.

Keeping users oriented across a six-step technical pipeline

Every screen carries the same anchors, so no matter how technical the underlying step is, the user always knows where they are and what to do next.
