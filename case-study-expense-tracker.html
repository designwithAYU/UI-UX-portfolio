01 — Problem

People know what they spend. They don't know where it went.

Users often know how much money they spend but struggle to understand where their money goes — expenses are logged but rarely turned into a pattern anyone can act on.

07 — Responsive Design

One dashboard, two very different layouts

On desktop, spending charts and recent transactions sit side by side. On mobile, the layout collapses into a single column — balance first, then a compact chart, then a scrollable transaction list — with a bottom tab bar replacing the side navigation.
