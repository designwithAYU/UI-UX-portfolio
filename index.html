Get to know me

A little about me

I'm a Computer Science student with a growing focus on UI/UX and Product Design. What draws me to design is the chance to solve real problems people face every day — and to see a confusing flow become something someone can use without a second thought.

My technical background means I can speak the same language as developers, understand what's feasible to build, and design with those constraints in mind from the start.

I'm early in my design career, and I like it that way — I take feedback seriously, iterate quickly and treat every project as a chance to get sharper.
