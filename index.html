A little about me

I'm a Computer Science student and junior UI/UX designer, working toward the combination of UX thinking, UI design and frontend understanding.

I like starting with the problem, not the screen — mapping out how something should work before deciding how it should look. Knowing how to build what I design also means I make fewer decisions that would be difficult to actually ship.

I'm early in my design career, and I take feedback seriously. Every project here is a step in getting sharper.
