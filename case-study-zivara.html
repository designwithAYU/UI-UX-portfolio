01 — Overview

What Ledgerly does

Ledgerly lets people log expenses, sort them into categories, and see where their money went each month — without opening a spreadsheet.

02 — Problem

Logged, but not understood

Expense tracking can become difficult when users have to interpret many transactions and spending categories on their own. The design goal was to create a simple dashboard that makes financial information easy to understand at a glance.

07 — Design Decisions

A few choices worth explaining

01

Balance leads every layout

On both desktop and mobile, the current balance is the first thing visible, before any chart or transaction list.

02

One chart per screen, not five

Rather than a chart-heavy overview, each analytics screen focuses on a single question — category breakdown, or trend over time — not both at once.

03

Adding an expense stays a single screen

Amount, category, date and notes all live on one form so logging a purchase doesn't take more than a few taps.

04

Bottom navigation on mobile, side navigation on desktop

Each platform gets the navigation pattern its users already expect, rather than one layout stretched across both.

Responsive Screens

One dashboard, two layouts

On desktop, spending charts and recent transactions sit side by side. On mobile, the layout collapses into a single column with a bottom tab bar replacing the side navigation.
