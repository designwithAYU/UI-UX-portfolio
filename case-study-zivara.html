02 — The Problem

Wellness apps meant to help often end up feeling like homework.

Users often struggle to maintain consistent mental wellness routines because existing wellness applications can feel overwhelming, complicated or difficult to use regularly. The goal was to remove that friction entirely.

03 — Design Goal

Create a simple, calming experience that lets users:

01

Check in with their mood

02

Understand their emotional patterns

03

Access wellness activities quickly

04

Track progress over time

05

Build healthy daily routines

08 — Wireframes

Low-fidelity first, so structure could be tested before style

Early wireframes focused purely on hierarchy — what needed to be seen first, and what could wait. The mood check-in moved from a buried tab to a primary card on Home once early feedback showed people missed it entirely.

12 — Usability Testing

What changed after testing

Sample findings from a project exercise, not a commercial usability study.
Finding

Users were unsure where to start a mood check-in.

Change

Made the mood check-in CTA more prominent on the home screen.

Finding

The insights screen felt like a spreadsheet at first glance.

Change

Replaced raw numbers with a simple weekly trend chart.

Finding

Some testers didn't notice the streak indicator.

Change

Moved the streak tag next to the greeting at the top of Home.
